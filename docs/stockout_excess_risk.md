# Stockout and Excess-Risk Analytics

The governed daily risk mart is `mart_stock_risk_daily`, generated by `src/transforms/inventory_risk_flags.py`. SQL consumers can use `vw_stock_risk_daily` and `vw_stock_risk_exceptions` from `marts/inventory/stock_risk_views.sql`.

## Grain and inputs

Grain is one SKU × store × business date. The mart uses `available_inventory_qty`, `on_hand_qty`, rolling `average_daily_demand_qty` and `demand_status`, `lead_time_days`, `safety_stock_days`, `target_stock_qty`, `unit_cost`, and product status. Rolling demand must be produced by [the Day 11 transformation](rolling_demand_days_of_supply.md) before risk scoring and joined to the daily inventory fact at its full SKU-store-date key. The SQL view expects that published demand result as `fact_inventory_demand_daily`.

## Governed flags

| Flag | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| `active_stockout_flag` | Product is active and available inventory is `≤ 0`. It remains detectable even if demand is zero or unavailable. |
| `stockout_risk_flag` | Active product with valid positive demand and days of supply `< lead_time_days + safety_stock_days`. |
| `low_cover_flag` | Active product with valid positive demand and days of supply `< lead_time_days + safety_stock_days + 7 days`. This includes stockout-risk records. |
| `excess_inventory_flag` | Active product with available inventory greater than target stock. Excess quantity is `max(available inventory - target stock, 0)` and excess value multiplies quantity by unit cost. |

Target stock follows the KPI contract: demand through lead time plus safety-stock cover, unless an approved positive source target is populated. When no source target is supplied, the mart derives it as `average_daily_demand_qty × (lead_time_days + safety_stock_days)`. The seven-day low-cover buffer follows the launch DOS threshold in [the KPI catalog](kpi_catalog.md).

## Missing inputs and precedence

- Zero, unavailable, or incomplete rolling demand yields null DOS. The active-stockout flag still evaluates; stockout-risk and low-cover flags remain false until demand is usable.
- Missing lead time or safety stock prevents stockout-risk and low-cover evaluation and produces a visible setup exception. A missing target is derived by the rule above when demand and parameters are available. Missing unit cost leaves excess quantity available but flags excess value as unavailable.
- Negative risk inputs are rejected into the mart's `INPUT_EXCEPTION` output rows, with reason retained.
- Inactive items receive no active risk flags.
- `risk_reason` precedence is inactive product, active stockout, unavailable/zero demand, stockout risk, low cover, then adequate cover.

## Persistence and use

The builder writes `mart_stock_risk_daily.csv`, `stock_risk_input_exceptions.csv`, and `stock_risk_manifest.json` under an immutable `run_id=<id>` partition. The SQL views provide the equivalent reusable logic for warehouse deployment. Publication is gated on successful Day 6 quality controls and the upstream rolling-demand output.
